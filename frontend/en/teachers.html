Ideas for the classroom

  • 📆

    Daily challenge as a warm-up

    The first 5 minutes of class: one problem per subject, the same for everyone at the same level. Then discuss the strategies.

  • 🧩

    Review before a test

    Smart review picks the skills each student is forgetting. Great for homework.

  • 🎯

    Groups by need

    Use "what the class most needs to practice" to form groups and revisit a topic with the students who need it.

  • 🏆

    Weekly goals

    Agree on a goal (e.g. 3 stars on the fractions stage) and track it in the class report.

  • 🧠

    Logic and computational thinking

    Sequences, sudoku, true or false, propositions and step-by-step programs.

  • ♿

    For the whole class

    Read-aloud, large text and reduced animations. Everyone starts at the level they're at.

Questions from teachers

How much does it cost to use with my class?

Creating a class, following your students and playing are free, with no ads. The Teacher Pro plan (R$19.90 per month) unlocks up to 20 classes, more assignments, class spreadsheets and printable worksheets.

Do I need to register my students?

No. You create the class and share the code. Each family adds their child's profile to the class from the Parent area. You don't receive students' full names, emails or photos, only nickname and progress.

Is the content mapped to a curriculum?

Yes. Every skill in the game is mapped to the related skills of Brazil's national curriculum (BNCC), for Mathematics (Grades 1 to 9 and some high school) and Computing (computational thinking). The full list is public and shows the grade of each skill.

Does it work in the computer lab and on phones?

Yes. It runs in the browser on computers, tablets and phones, with nothing to install.
