How it works

  1. 1

    Choose a nickname

    No forms: pick an animal for your avatar and start. If you already know a lot, take a quick test and jump to the right level.

  2. 2

    Solve short challenges

    3 to 5 minute sessions. The game picks problems at just the right level: not too easy, not impossible. There's a hint whenever you need one.

  3. 3

    Level up

    Earn stars, unlock new regions of the map and, when the bar reaches 85%, face the Guardian Challenge to level up.

From the first step to the top of the mountain

The level comes from performance, not age. Every child moves at their own pace.

🌱 Beginner

Math: counting, tens, addition and subtraction, equal groups, money and time.

Logic: patterns, sequences, "which one is different?", greater and less.

🌿 Intermediate

Math: times tables, division, expressions, fractions, decimals, perimeter and area.

Logic: 4×4 Sudoku, magic square, balance scales, true or false.

🌳 Advanced

Math: negative numbers, powers, LCM and GCD, proportion, equations.

Logic: 6×6 Sudoku, propositions, sets, detective puzzles.

🏔️ Expert

Math: systems, quadratics, interest, Pythagoras, counting.

Logic: truth tables, knights and knaves, step-by-step programming.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. Playing is free, with no ads and no in-game purchases. The Family and Teacher Pro plans are optional and add features for the adults who follow along, like the weekly report and printable worksheets.

Do I need an account?

Not to get started: a nickname is enough, and progress is saved on the device. To avoid losing it and to play on other devices, just use "Save progress" and create the account whenever you like — everything already earned stays.

What age is it for?

From age 7, with no upper limit: kids, teens and adults play together. Since the level comes from performance, everyone starts where they are; the placement test helps find the starting point.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer. You can also install it on the home screen like an app.

What data do you keep?

Only nickname, avatar, school grade (optional) and game progress. The account belongs to the responsible adult, who can delete everything at any time. Teachers only see the profiles the family adds to the class.
