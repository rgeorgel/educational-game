Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This is a translation of the original policy in Portuguese. If there is any difference between the versions, the Portuguese version prevails.

Summary in plain language

  • For children, we keep only what the game needs to work: nickname, avatar, school grade (if you provide it) and progress on the challenges. No full name, photo, location or contacts.
  • No ads and no trackers in the game. We don't sell or rent data, and we don't use children's data for advertising. Only the information pages and the blog use Google Analytics, and only if you accept.
  • The responsible adult decides: they create the profiles, choose whether the child joins a class and can delete everything at any time under Settings.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store the card number.

1. Who takes care of your data

Educa Game (educa-game.georgel.ca) is the controller of the data processed in the game, under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, “LGPD”).

  • Responsible party: Ricardo Georgel, CPF (Brazilian taxpayer ID) 368.656.898-52
  • Contact and data protection officer (DPO): georgel.ricardo.g@gmail.com

2. What data we process

WhoseData
Responsible adult or teacher (account) Email, password (stored only as an irreversible hash, never as text), the date these terms were accepted, and the dates of creation and last access.
Child (player profile) Nickname, chosen avatar and accessories, year of birth and school grade (optional) and progress: answers, correct answers, response time, hints used, XP, levels, stars and medals.
Guest (playing without signing up) Nickname, avatar and progress, without an email. They are linked only to the device, through a cookie.
Classes Class name, join code, which profiles joined and the assignments created by the teacher.
Site usage On which days the account opened the game (to count visits) and where the first visit came from (for example, a campaign link or the referring site), without identifying the person on other sites.
Subscription (paid plans) Plan, subscription dates and status, and the customer ID at Stripe. Card details are provided directly to Stripe.
Technical logs IP address and date and time of access, kept for security and for the period required by Brazil's Internet Civil Framework (6 months).

What we don't collect: children's full names, photos, voices, locations, contacts or documents. We ask that nicknames and class names also don't include full names.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • Running the game and saving progress: choosing the next challenges, adjusting the difficulty to the child's level, unlocking stages, medals and levels. Basis: specific consent of the parent or guardian (art. 14, §1, LGPD) and performance of the service (art. 7, V).
  • Showing reports to the adult: the parent report, the weekly report and the class report for the teacher. Same basis.
  • Sending account emails: welcome, link to create a new password, password-changed notice and subscription notices. Basis: performance of the service (art. 7, V).
  • Charging for paid plans and issuing tax documents. Basis: performance of the contract and legal obligation (art. 7, V and II).
  • Security and abuse prevention (login attempt limits, technical logs). Basis: legal obligation and legitimate interest (art. 7, II and IX).
  • Measuring visits to the information pages and the blog with Google Analytics (how many people visit and where they come from). Only on those pages, never in the game. Basis: consent (art. 7, I), given in the cookie notice and which can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Improving the game with aggregate numbers (for example, which skills are too hard), without identifying anyone. Basis: legitimate interest (art. 7, IX), always in the best interest of the child.

We don't use data for advertising, commercial profiling or automated decisions that affect rights. The difficulty adjustment is used only for the game.

4. Children's data

Educa Game was made for children, always with a responsible adult. Following article 14 of the LGPD:

  • Only an adult (18 or over) can create an account and, by doing so, declares that they are responsible for the children who will play and gives consent.
  • Guest mode keeps only nickname and progress on the device. To save progress to an account, a parent or guardian must authorize it.
  • We ask only for the data strictly necessary for the game. No game feature depends on extra personal data.
  • The game has no chat, no messages between players and no ads.

5. Teachers and classes

When a child joins a class with the teacher's code, the teacher can see that child's nickname, avatar and performance on the skills (including BNCC codes) and on the class assignments. The teacher does not see the parent's email or the account's other profiles. The parent can remove the child from the class whenever they want, and the teacher can remove students or delete the class.

Teachers who use the game with students are responsible for following the school's rules and, when necessary, getting the parents' permission.

6. Who we share data with

We don't sell data. We share it only with companies that help us provide the service (processors), and only what is necessary:

  • Hosting and database: servers where the game and the data are stored.
  • Email delivery: the provider that delivers account emails (receives the adult's email address and the message text).
  • Stripe: processes payments for the plans (receives the adult's email and the payment details they provide). See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics: only if you accept in the cookie notice, and only on the information pages (home and teachers) and the blog. It receives browsing data (pages viewed, where you came from, device and browser type, approximate city). It receives no account data and nothing from the game. Google signals and ad personalization are turned off. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Authorities: when there is a legal obligation or court order.

Children's data (nickname and progress) is not sent to Stripe, the email provider or Google.

7. International transfer

Some processors, such as Stripe, the email provider and Google (Analytics), may process the adult's data outside Brazil. In these cases, we require appropriate safeguards, such as contractual clauses, in accordance with article 33 of the LGPD.

8. Cookies and storage on the device

  • Login cookie (edugame.auth): essential, keeps you signed in. For guests, it is what keeps progress on the device.
  • Browser local storage: preferences (language, theme, text size, read-aloud), the chosen profile and the origin of the first visit.
  • Cache to work with a weak connection: the game's own files.
  • Google Analytics (_ga and _ga_…, lasting up to 2 years): only on the information pages and the blog, and only after you click Accept in the cookie notice. Without acceptance, Google's script isn't even loaded. The game (/jogar) doesn't use these cookies.

Your choice about Google Analytics is stored in the browser. To change it, click Cookies in the footer of the home page or the blog; if you decline, we delete this site's Google Analytics cookies. We don't use advertising cookies.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account, profiles and progress: as long as the account exists. When you delete the account under Settings, we immediately delete the account, the profiles and all progress.
  • Guests: deleted automatically after 180 days without access.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in backups for a limited period, until they are replaced, without being used for any other purpose.
  • Technical logs: 6 months (Brazil's Internet Civil Framework).
  • Subscriptions and tax documents: for the period required by tax law (usually 5 years).

10. Your rights

As the data subject, or the child's parent or guardian, you can request at any time (art. 18 of the LGPD):

  • confirmation that we process your data and access to it;
  • correction of incomplete or wrong data;
  • anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data;
  • data portability;
  • deletion of data processed with consent and withdrawal of consent;
  • information about who we share data with.

How to do it: to delete everything, use Settings → Delete my account and all data in the game. Profiles can also be deleted one by one. For other requests, write to georgel.ricardo.g@gmail.com. We reply within 15 days. You can also file a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

11. Security

We use an encrypted connection (HTTPS), passwords stored only as an irreversible hash, cookies protected from script access, login attempt limits and restricted staff access to data. No system is 100% secure: if an incident happens that could bring relevant risk or harm, we will notify those affected and the ANPD, as the law requires.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date of the last update is at the top of the page. When the change is relevant, we will let you know by email or in the game itself before it takes effect.

See also the terms of use.
